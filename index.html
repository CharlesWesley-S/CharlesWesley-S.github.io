What I believe

◇
01

Cybersecurity should protect people, not only machines.

Real attacks combine technology with psychology. The next generation of security should be proactive, context-aware, and human-centered.

⌬
02

AI agents need autonomy with boundaries.

Useful autonomy requires permission control, traceable actions, human confirmation, and a clear understanding of when to stop.

Quantum computing will reshape security gradually, but deeply.

Quantum is not magic. Its importance lies in changing what is computationally possible and forcing us to rethink cryptographic foundations.
